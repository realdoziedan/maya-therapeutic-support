# Review and delivery notes

## Review of the handoff
The original is comprehensive and preserves a thoughtful therapeutic voice, multimodal references, optional records, faith-by-consent, two-tier relationship response, and practical platform adapters. Areas needing revision: quiet drive search conflicts with informed consent; “most people played a part” risks blaming abuse survivors; Tier 1 accountability needs explicit asymmetry; safety information must not be stored in discoverable/shared accounts; hotline number hours/cost were not consistently supported by primary sources; platform availability can change; screening tools need non-diagnostic limits and exact validated wording.

## Changes made
- Reorganized into platform-neutral core, references, source-text locations, templates, adapters and fictional review scenarios.
- Made record access/writes/sharing opt-in, scoped, privacy-aware and especially cautious for couples/safety records.
- Kept faith-based sowing/reaping and reciprocal action as substantive meaning-making lenses, including reflection on subtle personal contribution, by consent and without dismissing them through a contemporary label.
- Clarified that personal reflection and another person’s responsibility for betrayal/abuse can both be true; avoid shame, unjustified spiritual certainty, and using reflection to excuse harm.
- Balanced betrayal repair: the injured partner’s reassurance needs are valid; freely agreed, bounded transparency can be discussed alongside privacy and emotional impact for both. If declined, Maya explores why, offers alternative repair steps, and helps both name their boundaries and any impasse without coercion.
- Added a structured way to examine direct/indirect consequences of a person’s own actions, while separating their contribution from other people’s choices and wider circumstances.
- Consolidated records into a living Overview, dated session notes, and referral reports created on request; removed separate profile, care-plan, safety-plan, and couples-note templates. Safety planning is an optional section unless a standalone copy is useful.
- Added a standalone service-and-safety specification and a compact review suite covering referrals, crisis escalation, relationship safety, faith, record consent, repair refusal, and individual life concerns.
- Added a fictional personal-record fixture and one desk-simulated individual work-stress session with updated Overview, session note, sample dialogue, and results.
- Clarified Tier 1 accountability and Tier 2 safety-mode boundaries.
- Added medical emergency, monitored-device, minor safeguarding, tool-capability, screening and referral safeguards.
- Reviewed Nigerian contacts: retained clearly published She Writes Woman and SURPIN contacts; caveated Lagos numbers; directed users to current Find A Helpline listings. Sources and review date are in risk-and-safety.md.
- Added no copyrighted faith text; provided owner-managed source folders and rights instructions.
- Updated ChatGPT adapter for current GPT availability/migration status; platform details should be checked at installation.
