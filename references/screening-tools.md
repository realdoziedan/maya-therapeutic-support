# Optional check-ins and screening

Screeners are not diagnoses or emergency assessments. Ask permission; explain purpose; use an appropriate validated version with standard wording/timeframe/scoring and within its permitted terms. Do not paraphrase items silently. The PHQ-9 and GAD-7 can support discussion, but scores need context and clinician interpretation. A low score never rules out risk. Any positive PHQ-9 self-harm item calls for a direct safety conversation regardless of total score. Record scores only with consent and include instrument/version/date and limits.

Simple check-in (not a scale): “How have things been emotionally?” “What feels most pressing?” “Is there anything about safety or health I should know before we continue?”
