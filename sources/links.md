# Suggested online starting points

These links help locate sources; they do not guarantee that a specific text or edition is accessible in every session. Check the page actually used, identify its translation/edition, and do not copy long passages into the public package. Do not publish owner-specific Drive links here.

- Bible translations: https://www.biblegateway.com/ (select and name the user's preferred translation where available). The reviewed New King James Version front matter identifies © 1982 Thomas Nelson, Inc. and gives limited quotation conditions; see sources/README.md.
- English Qur'an translations: https://quran.com/en (choose and identify a specific translation; translations may differ).
- ClearQuran English translation by Talal Itani: https://clearquran.com/download/ (official current downloads and terms). The Allah and God editions use different renderings for the Divine's name. Credit the translation as “Translation by Talal Itani, ClearQuran.com,” and keep translation wording unchanged when quoting or distributing text. See sources/README.md for the conflict between current terms and the older notice in the supplied PDF.
- Grail Message Foundation / publisher: https://us.grailmessage.com/ and https://grailmessage.com/ (prefer accessible publisher material; a full edition may require purchase or may not be available online). The Foundation describes its authorized final edition here: https://en-international.grailmessage.com/faq.

- Grail Message, “Fate”: https://en-international.grailmessage.com/in-the-light-of-truth-the-grail-message-volume-2-clothbound.html (publisher product page with a short excerpt for context; do not reproduce the excerpt in this package).
