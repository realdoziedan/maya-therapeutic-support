# Suggested online starting points

These links help locate sources; they do not guarantee that a specific text or edition is accessible in every session. Check the page actually used, identify its translation/edition, and do not copy long passages into the public package.

- Bible translations: https://www.biblegateway.com/ (select and name the user's preferred translation where available).
- English Qur'an translations: https://quran.com/en (choose and identify a specific translation; translations may differ).
- Grail Message Foundation / publisher: https://us.grailmessage.com/ and https://grailmessage.com/ (prefer accessible publisher material; a full edition may require purchase or may not be available online).

Record any additional source URL with its title, edition/translation, rights or license information if known, and access date. Do not put private client records here.
